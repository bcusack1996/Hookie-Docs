Hookie keeps two things for every event it accepts:

- A **submission** is the body exactly as it arrived, before anything reshaped it. Every accepted request makes one, even when nothing is routed.
- A **record** is what routing produced from it: the whole payload, or the fields a rule or endpoint mapped out. A record belongs to one **dataset**.

One submission can make several records. A keyed event that matches three [rules](/concepts/rules-and-mappings) makes three records, one in each rule's dataset. It can also make none: an event no rule matches is still a submission, which is how you tell "no rule matched" from "the event never arrived".

## Datasets

A **dataset** is a named set of records, like a table. You never create one. A dataset exists the moment the first record is routed into it, so naming it in an endpoint, a rule or a workflow step is enough.

Dataset names are lowercase letters, digits and underscores. The console suggests one from the endpoint's name, with hyphens turned into underscores.

A dataset is also the unit that the rest of Hookie selects on:

- A [destination](/concepts/destinations-and-deliveries)'s dataset filter names the datasets it receives.
- A [workflow](/workflows) starts from its entry dataset.
- A [customer portal](/customer-portal) exposes chosen datasets as event types.
- The [Data API](/data-api) serves chosen datasets to your own code.

## Browse records

A project's **Datasets** tab lists each dataset with its record count and when it last received one. Choose **View records** to open one. The records are a table with one row per record. Choose **Refresh** to fetch records that arrived after the page loaded, and **Open record** on a row to see the whole record and the **request** it arrived in: the method, content type, source IP, query string and headers. Credentials such as `Authorization` and `Cookie` are stored as `[redacted]`.

**Export CSV** and **Export XLSX** download the whole dataset with every field, whatever view is on screen. A large export shows its progress and can be cancelled or resumed. An XLSX file stops at a sheet's limit of 1,048,575 rows.

On a very large dataset, the record count stops at 10,000 (shown as "10,000+ records"), and a view sorted by a payload field sorts only the newest 10,000 records. Sorting by arrival covers the whole dataset.

## Record views

A **view** is a saved way to look at a dataset: which fields matter, in what order, and what the columns are called. Views change only how records are shown; they never change the records.

With a dataset open, choose **New view**. Enter a **View name**, tick the fields to show as columns, reorder them, and rename any heading. Tick **Default view for this dataset** to open the dataset on this view. A dataset has at most one default view.

Choose a view from the list above the table to switch to it. **Edit view** and **Delete view** act on the view on screen. Deleting a view removes only the view; the records are untouched.

Connected agents use the same views through `list_record_views`, `create_record_view` and `query_records`, so a view you set up is the shape an agent reads too.

## Find the raw body

The project's **Observability** tab searches submissions, records, deliveries, AI runs and AI calls over a time range, the last 24 hours by default. Widen it with **Time range**, down to a custom window to the minute. Each kind of result has its own filters, such as a delivery's status, destination, response code and latency, or an event's dataset.

Paste any ID into **Trace an id** to see its whole chain: the submission, the records made from it, and every delivery of those records. That includes the `Hookie-Delivery-Id` your receiver logged. The filters are kept in the page address, so you can share a search as a link.

## How long records are kept

Records and submissions are kept for your plan's retention window, then deleted: 7 days on Free, 30 days on Pro and 90 days on Team. See [Limits and plans](/concepts/limits-and-plans). To keep data longer, send it to a [destination](/concepts/destinations-and-deliveries) of your own.

To remove data sooner, **Delete record** in a record's preview erases it with its submission, deliveries and the workflow runs it started (developers and up). **Purge dataset** in the dataset's menu on the **Datasets** list erases every record in it (owners and admins). See [Delete, export and close](/data-deletion).

## The `source` of a record

Each record carries where it came from. An event from an endpoint URL has the source `webhook:<endpoint id>`. The endpoint's slug is never used, because the slug is the endpoint's credential.
