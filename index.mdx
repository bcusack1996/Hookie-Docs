Hookie is one place to collect everything that gets sent to you: **ingest** any payload — JSON from a webhook provider, or a plain HTML form posting straight at a URL — **route** it into datasets with rules, and **deliver** signed events with retries, a live stream, AI triggers, and multi-step [workflows](/workflows). You can also [connect a coding agent](/mcp) and have it build the whole thing for you.

## The request lifecycle

Every ingested payload follows the same path:

1. Authenticate the ingest key (constant-time) and check the IP allowlist.
2. Apply the edge rate limit, then short-circuit on `Idempotency-Key`.
3. Enforce the monthly event quota for your plan.
4. Store the raw body verbatim in `submissions` — decoding form encodings into fields first, if that is what arrived.
5. Route into `records` via the identity dataset, your rules, or a webhook's mapping.
6. Fan out to outbound deliveries, the live stream, and any matching AI triggers — then start or resume any [workflows](/workflows) whose entry conditions match.
