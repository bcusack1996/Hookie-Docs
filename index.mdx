Hookie is a webhook platform: it gives you a URL to receive events, somewhere structured to keep them, logic to act on them, and a signed, retrying delivery out the other side. Everything runs on Cloudflare Workers, D1 and Durable Objects.

## How an event moves through Hookie

<Steps>
  <Step title="It arrives">
    Post to an endpoint's URL — the high-entropy slug in `app.hookie.ai/{workspace}/{project}/{webhook}` is the credential — or to `/v1/ingest/{key}` with an ingest key. JSON, form posts and multipart all work, so an HTML `<form>` can point straight at an endpoint with no JavaScript and no backend of your own.

    Before anything is stored, the request passes the endpoint's IP allowlist, its rate limit, an `Idempotency-Key` check and your plan quota.
  </Step>
  <Step title="It's captured, then routed">
    The raw body is stored as a **submission** — exactly as it arrived, before anything reshapes it — so "no rule matched" is always distinguishable from "the event never arrived."

    Addressed requests store the payload whole into the dataset the URL names. A keyed request with no dataset in the path is the one shape that consults **rules**: each enabled rule tests its conditions against the payload and, where they hold, maps fields out into a **record**. Rules are not first-match — one event can land in several datasets at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="It fans out">
    A new record triggers every matching **destination** (an outbound webhook, signed HMAC-SHA256, retried with backoff), pushes onto the **live stream** for anyone tailing it, and starts any **workflow** whose trigger it satisfies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="It gets acted on">
    A workflow runs steps in order: call a model, branch on the result, write to a dataset, emit a further event. Workflows also start on a schedule or from a WebSocket condition, not only from an inbound event.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's in the box

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Signed delivery" icon="signature" href="/signatures">
    Outbound webhooks carry an HMAC-SHA256 signature and a timestamp. Verify them in a few lines, in any language.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Live streaming" icon="radio" href="/streaming">
    Tail events in real time over SSE or WebSocket, with resume after a disconnect.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Customer portal" icon="users" href="/customer-portal">
    Let your own customers manage their webhook destinations and inspect their deliveries, without a support ticket.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Observability & search" icon="magnifying-glass" href="/quickstart#3-see-it-in-the-console">
    Search submissions, records and deliveries, and follow any id along the whole chain it belongs to.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Every resource — datasets, rules, endpoints, destinations, workflows — is scoped to a **project**, and every workspace starts with a Default project. If you are just trying things out, you can ignore projects entirely and use that one.
</Note>
